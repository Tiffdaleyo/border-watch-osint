Unofficial project, open-source news only. Not a government service and not linked to any agency's official systems. Items are found automatically and are leads to verify against the original article and official sources. Review priority = what to read first. It never means a person, country or region is risky. Priority flags are not evidence and never reflect nationality. Click any bar or map bubble to filter the whole dashboard.

        Unverified totals. One figure per report is added (the headline figure where there is one), and the same large figure reported again within 30 days counts once; background figures such as yearly totals, earlier cases or whole operations are left out, and repeat coverage of one case is grouped first, though some repeats may remain.             About the data: how the review priority works, and this week compared with usual

            Review priority = what to read first. It never means a person, country or region is risky. Only incidents (crimes) are scored; news with no specific crime (new visa rule, travel advisory, deportation flight, border system) is shown as “Update” and not scored. __BANDS__

            “Rising” in this table: at least 3 reports in the last 7 days and at least double the average week of the 4 weeks before. “Falling”: half or less of a usual week (of at least 2). A pattern to look into, not a finding.

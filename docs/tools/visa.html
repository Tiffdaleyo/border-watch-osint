Visa requirements

Does a holder of this passport need a visa for that country? Start with arrivals into Jamaica, or check any passport and destination.
Reference only, and it may be out of date. Visa rules change often (see the Visa & Entry Rules section of the dashboard for recent changes). Always confirm with the destination's official immigration authority or your agency's systems before deciding anything. Residence permits, other visas held and the purpose of travel can change the answer.

Meaning of the answers

Visa free no visa needed (with the allowed stay in days, where known) · eTA an electronic travel authorisation is needed before travel · e-Visa an online visa before travel · Visa on arrival issued at the border · Visa required a visa from an embassy or consulate before travel · No admission holders are not admitted.

Case playbooks

Step-by-step checklists for the four most common airport immigration cases: what to look for, what to check, which tools on this site help, and how to refer and record.
A memory aid, not a procedure. These steps are drawn from public guidance (ICAO Doc 9303, IOM, UNODC and OSCE indicator lists). Your agency's standard operating procedures, the law and your supervisor's instructions always come first.

Unofficial tool, part of Border Watch OSINT (open-source only, not a government service). Indicators are prompts to look closer; no single indicator proves anything.

Suspect profile

Build a one-page profile of a person of interest from what is known, with ready-made open-source searches for each name, phone, email, username or document.
Works only in your browser. Nothing you type is saved, sent or logged, and it is gone when you close or reload the page. Print or save the profile as PDF to keep it, and store it under your agency's rules. The search buttons open outside websites and send them the search words: use them only where your agency's rules for online enquiries allow, and never type case details into those sites. Green buttons search this site's own reports.
